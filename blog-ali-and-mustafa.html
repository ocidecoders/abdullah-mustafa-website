The Story Behind Ali & Mustafa

By Abdullah Al Mustafa • 3 min read

Every artist has a moment that changes everything. For me, it started with a deleted file. This is the story of how I discovered my musical idol, Ali Zafar.

The First Encounter

When I was a child, I somehow got my hands on a Naat by Ali Zafar — Balaghal Ula Bi Kamaalihi. I listened to it, but honestly? I didn't like it. My young ears weren't ready for that sound yet. So, without a second thought, I deleted it from my playlist.

The Day Without Internet

Time passed. One day, I found myself completely alone with no network on my phone. There was nothing to do. Out of sheer boredom, I went into my phone's download folder to see what old music I had saved.

To my surprise, I found the folder where I had thrown away all the "deleted" files. Balaghal Ula Bi Kamaalihi was still sitting there.

"I restored it, pressed play, and listened. Then I heard Shararat. And suddenly, everything changed."

The Change of Heart

With nothing else to do, I gave the Naat another listen. This time, it felt different. I restored the whole folder, and then I heard Shararat. The melody, the vibe—it just clicked. I loved it.

At that moment, a burning curiosity hit me: "Who is Ali Zafar?"

The Deep Dive

I immediately started searching for him. I listened to Jhoom, then Julie, and then the anthem Rockstar. Each song was better than the last. I was completely blown away by his versatility, his lyrics, and his energy.

What started as a deleted file ended up becoming one of my biggest musical inspirations. This journey is why I always say: Never completely close the door on a song. Sometimes, the music you need finds you exactly when you need it the most.

Listen to my music, heavily inspired by this journey, on YouTube.
