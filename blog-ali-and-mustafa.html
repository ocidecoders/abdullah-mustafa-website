The Story Behind Ali & Me

Written by Abdullah Al Mustafa • 3 min read

Today, I want to share a very personal memory with you. It’s a story about how I discovered my biggest musical inspiration, Ali Zafar. And it all started because of a song I absolutely hated as a kid.

The Deleted Naat

When I was much younger, I somehow got my hands on a Naat by Ali Zafar — Balaghal Ula Bi Kamaalihi. I listened to it once, and honestly? I didn't like it at all. My young mind just couldn't connect with the melody. So, without giving it a second thought, I deleted it from my playlist and moved on.

A Boring Day and an Old Folder

Time went by. Then, one day, I found myself completely alone with no internet connection. I was bored out of my mind. With nothing else to do, I started digging through my phone’s old download folders, looking for any music I might have forgotten about.

That’s when I found the "Deleted" folder. And sitting right there was Balaghal Ula Bi Kamaalihi.

"Out of pure boredom, I restored the file. I pressed play. And this time, it hit differently."

Shararat and the Search

I listened to it again, and suddenly, the melody made sense to me. I started exploring the folder more and found Shararat. I played it, and I absolutely loved it. The vibe, the music, the voice—it was magic.

At that exact moment, a massive curiosity took over me. I needed to know: Who is Ali Zafar?

Jhoom, Julie, and Rockstar

I immediately started searching for him. I listened to Jhoom. Then Julie. And then, of course, Rockstar. Every single track blew my mind. I was amazed by how versatile he was.

What started as a deleted file ended up becoming the biggest influence on my own music. It taught me a valuable lesson: sometimes, you just aren't ready for a song yet. But when the time is right, that exact same song will find you.

— Mustafa
