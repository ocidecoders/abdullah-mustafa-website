Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you want to know
Who is Abdullah Al Mustafa?

Abdullah Al Mustafa is a Bangladeshi singer, lyricist, and composer. He is also the founder of the digital agency Ocide Coders. Learn more on the page.

Why was "Noor Tera Kaafi" removed from YouTube?

The song was removed due to an unforeseen incident. Mustafa is currently re-creating the song to deliver an even more refined and polished version for his listeners.

What languages does Mustafa sing in?

Mustafa primarily writes and sings in Hindi and Urdu. His music spans contemporary pop, Sufi, Qawwali, and devotional (Hamd) genres.

Who are his musical influences?

He is heavily inspired by Ali Zafar, Atif Aslam, Nusrat Fateh Ali Khan, and the Sabri Brothers. He deeply respects their contribution to South Asian music.

What is Ocide Coders?

Ocide Coders is a web development agency founded by Mustafa in 2024. They build websites, portfolios, and digital products like Eliget and meeEL. You can visit them at ocidecoders.onrender.com.
