General Information

Who is Abdullah Al Mustafa singer?

Abdullah Al Mustafa is a Bangladeshi singer, lyricist, and composer. He is a self-taught musician who writes and performs primarily in Hindi and Urdu, blending contemporary pop with Sufi and devotional elements. He is also the founder of the digital agency Ocide Coders.

What is Abdullah Al Mustafa's biography?

Born in Cox's Bazar and based in Chattogram, Mustafa began writing songs at sixteen and composing at seventeen. His debut single was Noor Tera Kaafi, released in March 2026. Read the full biography here.

Is Abdullah Al Mustafa releasing a new song in 2026?

Yes. After the removal of his debut single, Mustafa is currently re-creating Noor Tera Kaafi to deliver a refined version. He is also working on multiple new tracks like GulBahar and Tum Jaisa.
