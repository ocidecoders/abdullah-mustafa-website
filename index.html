About

Abdullah Al Mustafa is a Bangladeshi singer, composer and lyricist. His work is rooted in Hindi-Urdu music - a language of expression that travels beyond borders. Every song is written and composed by him, carrying personal stories, quiet emotions, and an unmistakable melodic signature.

With an upcoming single titled GulBahar, he continues to build a body of work that speaks to listeners across South Asia and beyond.
