About the Artist

A poet with a microphone.

Abdullah Al Mustafa is a Bangladeshi singer, composer, and lyricist whose music lives in the Hindi-Urdu tradition. He writes, composes, and performs his own songs, treating each one as a small piece of storytelling rather than a track to be consumed and forgotten.

His work is shaped by poetry, restraint, and a belief that a song should feel like a conversation rather than a performance. His upcoming single, GulBahar, marks his most personal release to date.

- Abdullah Al Mustafa
