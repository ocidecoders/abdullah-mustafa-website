Bio

Abdullah Al Mustafa is a singer, writer, lyricist, and composer. He is a self-taught musician, inspired by Ali Zafar, and he deeply respects the Sabri Brothers, Nusrat Fateh Ali Khan, and Atif Aslam.

At sixteen, he dreamed of becoming a musician. At seventeen, he began working for it.

His debut single, Noor Tera Kaafi — a Hamd — was released on YouTube on March 22, 2026. Mustafa wrote it, composed it, and sang it himself. Hasib Raihan produced it, and Mustafa did the mixing and mastering. Due to an unforeseen incident, the song was later removed from YouTube. He is now re-creating it to give his best.

Abdullah is currently working on multiple new songs. His music blends spiritual devotion with contemporary influences, aiming to connect with listeners through meaningful lyrics and soulful melodies.
