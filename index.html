01 — About

A voice shaped by two languages, one feeling.

Abdullah Al Mustafa is a Bangladeshi singer, composer, and lyricist whose music lives in the richness of Hindi–Urdu. Blending soulful melodies with honest writing, he crafts songs that feel both familiar and new.

From the first hum of an idea to the final mix, Abdullah writes, composes, and performs — treating each track as a small story worth telling.
