About

Who

Abdullah Al Mustafa is a singer, writer, lyricist, and composer.

He is a self-taught musician, inspired by Ali Zafar, and he deeply respects the Sabri Brothers, Nusrat Fateh Ali Khan, and Atif Aslam.

Work

  • Roles — Singer, Writer, Lyricist, Composer
  • Languages — Hindi, Urdu
  • Genres — Devotional (Hamd), Contemporary
  • Debut Single — Noor Tera Kaafi (a Hamd), released on YouTube, March 22, 2026
  • Current — Working on multiple new songs
  • Also known as — Mustafa

Journey

At sixteen, he dreamed of becoming a musician. At seventeen, he began working for it.

His debut single, Noor Tera Kaafi — a Hamd — was written, composed, and sung by him. It was produced by Hasib Raihan, with mixing and mastering by Mustafa. Due to an unforeseen incident, the song was later removed from YouTube. He is now re-creating it to give his best.

Writings

Beyond music, Abdullah writes — lyrics, poetic lines, and reflections. Every song begins as words on a page.
