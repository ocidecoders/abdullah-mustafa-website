Introduction

Abdullah Al Mustafa is a singer, lyricist, and composer from Chattogram.

His musical interests encompass contemporary Pakistani pop, Sufi music, Qawwali, and devotional music. He began writing songs at the age of sixteen and subsequently developed an interest in composition and independent music production.

Early Life & Musical Beginnings

Abdullah Al Mustafa's interest in music developed during his early years. At sixteen, he began writing original songs, laying the foundation for his development as a lyricist and aspiring musician. At seventeen, he began composing music for his earlier work, further expanding his involvement in the creative process.

His early songwriting included GulBahar, a song he wrote at sixteen and composed music for at seventeen. Although it was never professionally recorded or officially released, the piece represents an early milestone in his development as a songwriter and composer.

Alongside his musical pursuits, Abdullah has continued his education in the Science stream while refining his musical abilities through self-directed learning, experimentation, and hands-on experience.

Musical Career

Debut Release

Abdullah made his debut with Noor Tera Kaafi, a Hamd released on YouTube on 22 March 2026. He wrote, composed, and performed the piece, while Hasib Raihan served as its producer. Abdullah also undertook the mixing and mastering, contributing to several stages of the production process.

Following an unforeseen incident, the track was removed from YouTube. Abdullah subsequently decided to recreate the song, with the intention of producing a more refined version that better reflected his artistic vision.

Songwriting & Poetry

Songwriting and poetry form important parts of Abdullah's creative practice. In addition to his early work, GulBahar, he has written numerous songs, several of which remain unreleased.

His written poetry includes Tumara Nam, Kuch Hua Tha, and Tum Jaisa Khoob. Through songwriting and poetry, he explores emotions, personal experiences, and ideas that inform his approach to musical expression.

Abdullah continues to work on original compositions and songs as he develops his artistic identity and prepares material for future releases.

Musical Style & Influences

Abdullah's musical interests span contemporary Pakistani pop and traditional Sufi and Qawwali music. Among the artists who have influenced him are Ali Zafar, Atif Aslam, Nusrat Fateh Ali Khan, and the Sabri Brothers.

His favourite songs include Afreen Afreen, performed by Nusrat Fateh Ali Khan and Rahat Fateh Ali Khan; Aadat, associated with Atif Aslam; and Rockstar, by Ali Zafar.

Atif Aslam has played a particularly significant role in Abdullah's musical interests. The first Pakistani song he heard was by Atif Aslam — an experience that helped spark his lasting interest in Pakistani music.

He also draws inspiration from the vocal traditions and devotional expression associated with Sufi and Qawwali music, reflecting a range of influences in his musical interests.

Artistic Aspirations

One of Abdullah's long-term ambitions is to collaborate with Atif Aslam and Ali Zafar. His admiration for both artists dates back to childhood, when he watched them perform Yaar Yaaron Se Hona Juda.
