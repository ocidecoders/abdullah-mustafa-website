Who is Abdullah Al Mustafa?

By Ocide Coders • 3 min read

If you are searching for "Who is Mustafa" or "Bangladeshi Mustafa", you have arrived at the right place. Abdullah Al Mustafa, often known simply as Mustafa, is a rising Bangladeshi singer, lyricist, and composer.

Early Life and Background

Born in Cox's Bazar and currently based in Chattogram, Mustafa is a self-taught musician. At the age of sixteen, he began writing his own songs, and by seventeen, he was composing music. His early work includes the unreleased track GulBahar.

Musical Career and Debut

Mustafa made his official debut with Noor Tera Kaafi, a Hamd released on March 22, 2026. He wrote, composed, and performed the track himself. While the original release faced some unforeseen circumstances, he is currently working on a refined version of the song.

His musical influences include legendary artists like Ali Zafar, Atif Aslam, Nusrat Fateh Ali Khan, and the Sabri Brothers. He specializes in Hindi and Urdu tracks, blending contemporary pop with Sufi and Qawwali elements.

Beyond Music: The Founder of Ocide Coders

Aside from his music, Mustafa is also a tech visionary. In 2024, he founded Ocide Coders, a web development agency that builds premium digital solutions and platforms like Eliget and meeEL.

Where to Listen

You can listen to Abdullah Al Mustafa's music on his Official YouTube Channel. His poetry and writings are available on the Writings page of this website.
