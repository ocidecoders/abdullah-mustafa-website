The Musical Journey of Abdullah Al Mustafa

Official Biography • 4 min read

Abdullah Al Mustafa is a rising Bangladeshi singer, lyricist, and composer known for his versatile vocal range and deep connection to South Asian music. This article outlines his official musical journey, his inspirations, and his debut in the industry.

Early Life and Musical Beginnings

Born in Cox's Bazar and currently based in Chattogram, Mustafa's relationship with music started at a very young age. He is a completely self-taught musician. At just sixteen years old, he began writing his own original songs. By seventeen, he had already started composing music, showing a natural talent for both lyrics and melody.

The Debut: Noor Tera Kaafi

Mustafa made his official debut with Noor Tera Kaafi, an Islamic Hamd released on YouTube on March 22, 2026. He wrote the lyrics, composed the music, and performed the vocals himself. The track was produced by Hasib Raihan, with Mustafa handling the mixing and mastering. Due to unforeseen circumstances, the song was later removed, but Mustafa is currently recreating it to deliver an even better version to his listeners.

Musical Influences

His music is a blend of contemporary Pakistani pop, Sufi, and Qawwali. He draws heavy inspiration from legendary artists such as:

  • Ali Zafar (whose music deeply influenced his style)
  • Atif Aslam (the first Pakistani artist he listened to)
  • Nusrat Fateh Ali Khan
  • The Sabri Brothers

Beyond Music: The Tech Founder

Aside from his musical talents, Mustafa is also a tech entrepreneur. In 2024, he founded Ocide Coders, a web development agency. Under his leadership, the team has built innovative platforms like Eliget (an anti-addiction social network) and meeEL (a natural language web builder). This unique blend of art and technology sets him apart in both fields.

Future Aspirations

Abdullah Al Mustafa is currently working on multiple new Hindi and Urdu tracks. His goal is to connect with listeners through meaningful lyrics and soulful melodies. For official updates on his music, you can visit the Music section or check his YouTube Channel.
